Common Questions

Emergency Contact FAQ

Need immediate help? Check our rapid answers or reach out via our 24/7 hotline.

How fast can blood be delivered to an emergency surgery?

Within our metropolitan partner network, guaranteed emergency courier dispatch averages 18 minutes from receipt of doctor authorization.

Do I need an appointment to donate blood?

Walk-ins are welcomed at our Central HQ and all mobile coaches. However, booking an appointment ensures zero waiting time and guaranteed bed allocation.
