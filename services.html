Comprehensive Transfusion Medicine & Biologics

From emergency whole blood units to complex apheresis fractions, rare antigen registries, and cord blood stem cell cryobanking, Red Hope supplies complete clinical hemotherapy solutions.

Service 01

Whole Blood Phlebotomy & Component Fractionation

Standard 450ml whole blood donations are harvested using sterile, pyrogen-free quad-bag collection systems containing CPD-A1 anticoagulant solutions. Within 6 hours of donation, heavy centrifugation fractionates the pint into three therapeutic lifelines:

Packed Red Blood Cells (PRBC)

Stored at 2°C – 6°C for up to 42 days. Restores oxygen capacity in acute anemia, trauma, and orthopedic surgeries.

Platelet Concentrate (Random Donor)

Stored under continuous agitation at 20°C – 24°C for 5 days. Critical for acute thrombocytopenia.

Service 02

Apheresis Plateletpheresis (Single Donor Platelets)

Cancer chemotherapy and bone marrow transplants destroy the patient's ability to produce clotting platelets. Our Trima Accel apheresis suites draw blood, centrifuge out high-purity platelets, and return red cells and plasma back to the donor.

  • 1 SDP Donation = 6 to 8 Random Donor Units (significantly reduces recipient alloimmunization risk).
  • Rapid Recovery: Platelets replenish in 48 hours; donors can safely donate every 14 days (up to 24 times a year).
  • Bacterial Culture Screened: BacT/ALERT automated testing on every unit before hospital release.

Service 03

Fresh Frozen Plasma (FFP) & Cryoprecipitate

Blood plasma contains crucial albumin, immunoglobulins, and clotting factors. When frozen at -30°C within 8 hours of phlebotomy, it preserves Factor VIII, Factor IX, and Fibrinogen for patients experiencing massive hemorrhages, severe burn trauma, or liver failure.

Cryoprecipitated Antihemophilic Factor (Cryo)

A concentrated sub-fraction rich in Fibrinogen, Factor XIII, and von Willebrand factor. Used in acute obstetric DIC (Disseminated Intravascular Coagulation) and microvascular bleeding where low fluid volume is essential.

Service 04

Umbilical Cord Blood & Stem Cell Biobanking

Harvesting Hematopoietic Stem Cells (HSCs) at birth provides a 100% matched biological blueprint for life-saving cellular therapies and leukemia treatments.

Non-Invasive Delivery Collection

Collected painlessly in the delivery room right after umbilical cord clamping, without interfering with infant or mother care.

Liquid Nitrogen Vapour Storage

Cryopreserved at -196°C in robotic storage tanks with redundant electrical backup and 24/7 telemetry monitoring.

Public Donation or Private Reserve

Mothers can donate cord blood for free to the public registry to save a stranger, or enroll in private family storage.

Service 05

Specialized Immunohematology & Rare Blood Bank

Beyond ABO and Rh, human red blood cells feature over 340 recognized surface antigens. For patients with complex alloantibodies or rare phenotypes (such as Bombay Oh, Rh-null, or Duffy-null), standard blood will trigger fatal hemolytic reactions.

  • Extended Extended Antigen Phenotyping: Rh, Kell, Kidd, Duffy, and MNS screening.
  • Rare Donor Frozen Registry: Cryopreserved rare red cells stored with glycerol at -80°C for up to 10 years.
  • Consultative Crossmatch Services: 24-hour antibody identification reference laboratory.

Cost Accountability

Why Blood is Free, But Processing Incurs a Fee

As a 501(c)(3) non-profit, Red Hope never sells donated blood. Hospital reimbursement fees directly offset the hard clinical costs of individual Nucleic Acid Testing (NAT), blood bags, sterile tubing, licensed medical staff, and refrigerated courier transit.
